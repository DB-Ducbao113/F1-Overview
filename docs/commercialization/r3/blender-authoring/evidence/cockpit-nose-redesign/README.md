## Visual QA conclusion

N30 is the recommended direction. Its 0.665 m front-axle-to-opening clearance
preserves a readable nose/monocoque transition and a more coherent halo-to-
shoulder relationship. N20 is useful as the extreme study, but its 0.160 m
clearance compresses front suspension, cockpit and driver packaging into the
same visual zone and weakens premium composure.

Neither study is claimed as FIA-compliant. No high-detail, PBR, LOD, Showroom
integration or production replacement was performed.
