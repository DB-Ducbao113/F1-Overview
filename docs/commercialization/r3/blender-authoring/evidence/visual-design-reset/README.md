## Visual QA tied to evidence

### A — Aero Predator

- Aggressive identity and stance: the low shoulder, broad front-wing stack and
  long wedge side profile are the most overtly aggressive of the three
  ([side](aero-predator/side-clay.png), [front 3/4](aero-predator/front-three-quarter-clay.png)).
- Wing/nose coherence: the three progressively narrower swept elements visually
  feed the narrow nose; the endplates are still deliberately concept-stage.
- Sidepod/floor: a squared inlet, long tensioned shoulder and dark edge blade
  create the clearest separation from the floor.
- Rear/mechanical credibility: exposed double wishbones, open rims, twin rear
  supports and three diffuser dividers give depth, although the rear wing remains
  a simplified silhouette assembly ([rear 3/4 reflective](aero-predator/rear-three-quarter-reflective.png)).
- Originality/toy-like check: reads as an original arrow-shaped EDS proposal and
  is materially slimmer than Revision 2; the tall faceted engine spine requires
  owner direction before surface refinement.

### B — EDS Hyper Aero

- Aggressive identity and stance: the deepest undercut and largest shoulder
  volume create the strongest ground-effect visual architecture
  ([front 3/4 reflective](eds-hyper-aero/front-three-quarter-reflective.png)).
- Wing/nose coherence: the higher-dihedral three-element front wing gives a
  more sculpted relationship than A, while retaining the same locked nose tip.
- Sidepod/floor: paired shoulder and undercut character lines, the widest local
  floor plan and four rear channels make this the most aerodynamically expressive
  concept ([top clay](eds-hyper-aero/top-clay.png)).
- Rear/mechanical credibility: the high engine-cover peak and stronger diffuser
  rise make B visibly distinct at the rear, but the peak is currently too abrupt
  to call premium without owner-selected refinement.
- Originality/toy-like check: avoids Revision 2's inflated capsule sidepod; its
  risk is replacing bulbousness with an over-emphatic dorsal peak.

### C — Precision Machine

- Aggressive identity and stance: the lowest sidepod tail and clean horizontal
  shoulder produce a restrained, professional stance rather than maximum drama
  ([side reflective](precision-machine/side-reflective.png)).
- Wing/nose coherence: the only two-element front-wing concept; thinner elements
  and reduced dihedral give the clearest premium/technical hierarchy.
- Sidepod/floor: compact inlet, short controlled shoulder and narrower floor
  avoid the long inflated mass of Revision 2.
- Rear/mechanical credibility: lower engine spine, narrower rear wing and three
  disciplined diffuser dividers form the least theatrical rear composition
  ([rear clay](precision-machine/rear-clay.png)).
- Originality/toy-like check: the simplified surfacing is intentional restraint,
  not a primitive blockout; secondary aero density would only be added after the
  owner chooses this direction.

## Current visual defects / risks

- `EDS-SR-001` — **P2, all concepts:** rear wing/endplate architecture is still
  silhouette-grade and visually thin from exact rear. Expected: selected concept
  receives integrated supports and volumetric endplate treatment. Status: open,
  intentionally deferred until owner selection.
- `EDS-SR-002` — **P2, A/B:** engine-cover peaks are intentionally bold but may
  read too abrupt in side and rear-three-quarter views. Expected: tense continuous
  taper without a decorative fin impression. Status: owner direction required.
- `EDS-SR-003` — **P3, all concepts:** front endplates remain restrained planar
  masses at this gate. Expected: original sculpted wing-end treatment after the
  silhouette direction is fixed. Status: open, not hidden by material detail.
