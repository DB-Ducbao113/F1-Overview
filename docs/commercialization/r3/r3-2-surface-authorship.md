## Revision 1 continuity addendum (2026-10-21)

Owner Gate 2 rejected the separate-shell appearance of the R3.2 baseline
`a6549c6`. Revision 1 keeps all approved hardpoints, nose and wing envelopes,
wheelbase/track, and 5.050 x 2.000 x 1.180 m bounds. It replaces the independent

## Authorship

EDS-G03 R3.2 is an original Podium Effet De Sol design generated by the deterministic source in
`scripts/generate-eds-g03.mjs` and the dimensional registry in `scripts/eds-g03-design.mjs`.
The workflow is AI-assisted and uses analytic cross-sections, Catmull-Rom curve tubes,
super-elliptic body sections, NACA-derived wing thickness distributions and connected parametric
surface grids.

No C42 mesh, vertex positions, topology, UVs, texture, normal map, deformation or retopology was
used. Detailed + EDS was viewed only as a broad quality/proportion benchmark through the six
approved cameras. The generated GLB contains no textures and no imported branding.

## R3.2 surface decisions

- Nose/monocoque: one high-density centreline loft with a split lower keel, controlled shoulders
  and cockpit valley.
- Sidepods: independent crescent-entry spline lofts with an upper shoulder, lower undercut,
  waist and progressive rear taper; fairing patches connect the mass to the monocoque.
- Floor/diffuser: paired upper/lower Venturi surfaces, sealed lateral edges, channel contraction
  and a continuous diffuser rise inside the approved R3.1 envelope.
- Engine cover/airbox: multi-station central loft, inlet lip and restrained dorsal ridges blended
  toward the rear taper.
- Wings: closed curved airfoil volumes with spanwise arch, sweep, dihedral and camber; R3.1 wing
  envelopes remain locked.
- Halo: the approved centreline and envelope are unchanged; attachment fairings blend its three
  mounts into the survival cell.

## Inspection materials

The GLB carries five neutral, untextured inspection materials: body, aero, tyre, mechanical and
cockpit. The DEV viewer additionally offers a subtle reflective mode using runtime-owned
`MeshPhysicalMaterial` instances. It does not edit cached GLTF geometry or materials and disposes
only those owned runtime materials.
