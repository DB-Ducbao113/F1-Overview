Status: **owner visual review required**. EDS-G01 remains the commercial default.

## Source and method

- Design parameters: `scripts/eds-g02-design.mjs` (metres, front is negative Z).
- Editable surface/assembly source: `scripts/generate-eds-g02.mjs`.
- Export: `npm run asset:eds-g02` writes `public/models/eds-g02.glb` via Three.js GLTFExporter. No Blender installation was available in the local authoring environment. The source therefore uses custom cross-section lofts, curved airfoil and floor surfaces, radial tire profiles, rods and PBR material categories rather than modifying/subdividing G01 boxes.
- Validate: `npm run asset:eds-g02:validate` checks GLB structure, bounds, triangle winding, degenerate faces and normals. This is a repository validator, **not** an independent Khronos certification.
- The six existing EDS livery palettes are previewed through the development Showroom at `/showroom?model=eds-g02`. Without the query, G01 remains the default. The G02 GLB and preview URL are removed/disabled in the commercial build.

All geometry, palettes and material colours are repository-authored. The generator imports Three.js (MIT-licensed project dependency) and no third-party mesh, UV map, texture, logo or branding. The historical C42 model was used only as a visual-quality benchmark, not a geometry source. Original creation and absence of obvious logos do not automatically establish legal clearance; independent trade-dress and rights review remains necessary.

Remaining limitations visible in the renders: sidepod/engine-cover transitions can still appear detached or bulbous; the floor has broad simple surfaces; suspension and aero details remain a design study rather than production-grade mechanical surfacing. Owner feedback should determine whether to refine this candidate or move to a dedicated subdivision/NURBS authoring workflow. Do not promote G02 to default based on automated QA alone.
