## 1. Executive Summary & Classification Taxonomy

This manifest establishes a file-by-file compliance inventory for all media, 3D models, textures, vector graphics, raster imagery, maps, and typography in Podium Effet De Sol. Each asset is evaluated against intellectual property ownership, trade dress protection, commercial trademark laws, and copyright provenance.

### Classification Taxonomy:

- **KEEP**: Asset is 100% original, verified public domain, or compliant with commercial open-source licenses without trademark or trade dress encumbrances.
- **REVIEW**: Asset contains factual or editorial references that are defensible under nominative fair use or statutory data exemptions, but requires legal/policy monitoring during commercial scaling.
- **REPLACE**: Asset poses confirmed trademark, trade dress, copyright, or terms-of-use infringement risks and MUST be replaced prior to commercial monetization.
